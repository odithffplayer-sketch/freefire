Best Auto Headshot Sensitivity Settings for Free Fire 2026

If you play Clash Squad or Ranked in Free Fire, you already know one headshot can change the whole round. But landing consistent headshots isn't just about aim. Your sensitivity settings decide how fast your scope moves, how stable your drag is, and how easily you can pull "auto headshot" with SMG, AR, and Sniper.

Garena resets sensitivity for some players after updates, so here are the best auto headshot sensitivity settings for 2026 that pro players and content creators are using right now.

What Are Sensitivity Settings?

Sensitivity controls how fast your camera moves when you drag your finger. In Free Fire, higher sensitivity = faster flicks, lower sensitivity = more control. For auto headshot, we need a balance: fast enough to track, stable enough to lock on the head.

These settings work on both Android and iOS. If your phone lags, reduce DPI a little.

Best General Sensitivity Settings 2026

Here are the settings most top players use for auto headshot:

General: 95 - 100
This controls normal camera movement. Keep it high so you can rotate and check corners fast in Clash Squad.

Red Dot: 90 - 95
Used for close-range fights with MP40, Vector, and M1887. High red dot helps you drag straight to the head.

2x Scope: 85 - 90
Most AR fights happen on 2x. This range gives control + speed for head-level drags.

4x Scope: 80 - 85
For mid-range with SCAR, M4A1, and AK. Don't keep it too high or your scope will shake.

Sniper Scope: 65 - 75
AWM and Kar98 need stability. Low sensitivity helps you hold the crosshair on the head.

Camera: 100
This is for free-look while moving. Max it out so you can check 360 quickly.

Extra Settings That Boost Headshots

Sensitivity alone won't do the job. Change these too:

1. DPI Setting: 500 - 550
Go to Phone Settings > Developer Options > Smallest Width. Set DPI to 520. It makes drags smoother and more accurate. Don't go above 600 or touch will become too sensitive.

2. Button Size
Keep Fire Button size around 45-50 and place it where your thumb naturally rests. A misplaced button ruins drags.

3. Headshot Crosshair
Use a dot crosshair from settings. It helps you always aim at head level before you shoot.

4. Training
Spend 10 minutes in Training Ground with your sensitivity. Practice dragging from body to head with MP40. Muscle memory matters more than numbers.

Device-Wise Tips

Low-End Phones 2GB/3GB RAM: Reduce General to 85 and Red Dot to 80. High sensitivity causes lag and you'll miss drags.

60 FPS Phones: You can use full 100 sensitivity. The extra frames make drags very smooth.

iPhone Players: iOS touch is already fast. Start with General 90 and reduce if it feels too fast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Copying YouTuber settings blindly: Every phone and thumb is different. Use the numbers above as a base, then adjust +/- 5.

2. Changing settings before every match: Stick to one sensitivity for at least 1 week.

3. Aiming at the body: Always keep your crosshair at head level when running. Dragging from far away never gives auto headshot.

Final Verdict

There is no "one magic number" for auto headshot. But General 100, Red Dot 95, 2x 90, 4x 85, Sniper 70, Camera 100, DPI 520 is the 2026 meta setup that works for 90% of players.

Test it in custom room first. Do 50 drags with MP40, then 50 with M1887. If your bullets are landing on chest, increase Red Dot by 2. If scope is shaking, decrease it by 2.

Master this and you'll see more headshots, more Booyahs, and faster rank push in Clash Squad.
